Learnbay Career Navigator

A free, AI-guided career check on learnbay.co. In about 8 minutes, a working professional answers mostly tap-to-choose questions. The Navigator then builds their personal Career Blueprint: target roles, salary range, skills gap, a month-by-month roadmap, portfolio projects, and the Learnbay track that closes the gap.

The Blueprint is locked once it's built. It lives at a private link and is sent to the learner's email and WhatsApp. After that, the page is their home base. They can re-read the Blueprint, ask a scoped AI assistant, or book a counsellor. For Learnbay it does three jobs: it attracts leads, qualifies them, and gives us a nurture channel.

For the learner

"Tell me what AI means for my career, what to learn, and how long it takes." Answers that are specific to them, in minutes, for free.

For sales

A verified phone number plus about 25 structured fields, a readiness score, a stated goal and timeline, and a lead score, all before the first call.

For marketing

One destination for every ad, blog, and WhatsApp campaign, and a personal link we can keep sending people back to.

Product calls

Seven decisions that shape everything else

  1. 1. Structured first, AI second.About 80% of the session is taps: chips, sliders, and cards. The AI speaks at two points, a follow-up after the learner describes their pain points and the final Blueprint. Taps are faster and finish more often, cost almost nothing, and produce clean CRM fields. A fully open chat intake would be slower, more expensive, and would give us messy data.
  2. 2. Give value before asking for contact details, and ask before the full reveal.We ask for contact details at about 75% of the way through. The learner has already seen their AI Readiness Score and put in six minutes. The full Blueprint is what they're unlocking. Asking at the start kills completion. Asking at the end means people screenshot the result and leave.
  3. 3. A WhatsApp OTP is the only verification.One code, sent to the number they gave us. It proves the lead is real, confirms the channel we'll nurture on, and becomes their login later. Email isn't verified up front because the Blueprint link we send there confirms it.
  4. 4. Facts come from our database. Words come from the AI.Salary ranges, testimonials, placement stats, curriculum coverage, and project briefs are all lookups. The model picks from IDs we give it and writes the narrative around them. It never invents a number, a name, or a company. This is the biggest trust risk and the biggest legal risk in the product.
  5. 5. One Blueprint per person, locked.No editing the profile and no regenerating. A "Something looks off?" button creates a counsellor task instead. That turns a token cost into a sales conversation.
  6. 6. The assistant is scoped and metered.It answers from the learner's Blueprint and Learnbay's course knowledge base only, with a limit of 20 messages a day and 60 in total. Past the limit, and whenever a question is about fees, EMI, or placement guarantees, it hands over to a counsellor.
  7. 7. Built for mobile first.Most traffic will arrive from WhatsApp and paid social on phones. Every screen is designed at 375px first, one question per screen, with the thumb-zone CTA fixed at the bottom.
First visit · fully guided

The guided session

This is a linear flow with no navigation. It has one question per screen, a progress bar, and a back button. Answers are saved on the device, so a refresh doesn't wipe progress. The worked example throughout is Rahul, a QA engineer with 4 years in fintech.

  1. Hook

    0:00 · 1 screen

    "See what AI means for your career, and your exact path to the next role." One button: Build my Blueprint · 8 min. Below it are three real outcome lines from people with similar starting points. Traffic from ads or blogs arrives with a persona hint in the UTM, which pre-selects the likely role.

  2. Who you are

    ≈1:30 · 5 taps

    First name, current role, years of experience, industry, and current CTC band (with a "Prefer not to say" option). We ask for the first name only, so every later screen can address them by name.

    QA / TestingSoftware DevData / BIBusiness AnalystOps / Non-tech4–6 yrsBFSI / Fintech

  3. Where you want to go

    ≈1:00 · 2 taps

    "What do you want AI to do for your career?" Move into an AI/data role · Use AI in my current role · Move up to lead/manager · Change domain · Not sure, help me decide. Then a timeline: 3, 6, or 12 months, or "just exploring". The timeline answer is the strongest single intent signal we collect.

    Rahul: Move into an AI role, or use AI in testing (multi-select allowed) · within 6 months

  4. What's in the way

    ≈1:30 · taps + 1 AI turn

    Pain points, multi-select: Don't know where to start · No time · Weak in coding/maths · No real projects · Worried about layoffs · Started courses, never finished · Salary stuck · No interview calls. Then an optional box: "Anything else, in your words?" This is where the AI asks one or two follow-up questions. They are the only open-ended AI turns in the session.

    AI: "You said you've started courses before. What made you stop: time, difficulty, or it didn't feel relevant to testing?"

  5. AI Readiness Check

    ≈2:00 · 5 questions

    Five short scenario questions, not trivia. They cover Python, data handling, ML concepts, GenAI tool use, and "have you applied AI at work?". The output is shown immediately: a score out of 100, a band (Explorer · Builder · Practitioner), and an effort estimate in hours per week × months. This is the first payoff and the hook for the unlock step.

    Rahul: 42 / 100 · Builder · "About 8–10 hrs/week for 7 months to be interview-ready for your target role"

  6. Your call

    ≈1:00 · 3 taps

    This is where the need gets identified. "Based on your answers, here are three paths. Which feels right?" Each path card maps to a track. Then: "What would make you confident to switch?" (projects in my domain · a mentor · interview prep · a recognised certificate) and a preferred learning format. The Blueprint quotes their own choices back to them later, which keeps the pitch consistent with what they said.

  7. Unlock

    ≈0:40 · the contact step

    "Rahul, your Blueprint is ready to build. Where should we send it?" Fields: email and WhatsApp number (+91 by default). The consent wording sits right above the button: Send my Blueprint. A 6-digit code then arrives on WhatsApp, with SMS as a fallback. The Blueprint isn't generated until the number is verified, so bots and fake numbers cost us nothing.

  8. Build

    ≈0:30 · animated

    While the Blueprint generates, the screen shows honest progress steps ("Matching roles to your profile", "Mapping your skills gap", "Picking projects for fintech") and one testimonial from someone with a similar starting role. The wait is productive: they're reading proof while they wait.

  9. Reveal

    ≈8:00 · Blueprint

    The Blueprint opens, and the same link lands on WhatsApp and in email together with a PDF. From this point the flow ends and navigation opens up.

Returning visits

After the Blueprint: navigation

Once the Blueprint exists, the learner moves around freely. On mobile, sections are bottom tabs. On desktop they're a left rail. Talk to a counsellor is fixed on every screen.

Returning: the Blueprint link in WhatsApp or email opens it directly, as a signed link valid for 30 days. After that, a WhatsApp OTP logs them back in. There's no password.

Resume uploads happen only inside a verified session. PDF and DOCX only, 5 MB maximum. We extract the text on the server and delete the file within 7 days. We keep only the parsed text, with consent.

Why these lines matter commercially, not just ethically. India's CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns (2023) specifically name false urgency, confirm shaming, and forced action. ASCI expects placement and salary claims in education advertising to be substantiated. One screenshot of a fake timer on LinkedIn costs more than a month of leads.

These references are from memory: get legal to confirm the current rules before launch.

WhatsApp + email

Nurture engine

Every message points back to a section of their Blueprint, so each follow-up feels like a continuation of the help and not like a sales push. Lead temperature decides how fast we move.

Hot, 70 or more: counsellor call within 1 hour.  Warm, 40–69: WhatsApp nurture plus a counsellor within 24 hours.  Cold, under 40: email nurture, and they're promoted to warm when a behavioural trigger fires.

Default journey (warm lead)

  1. Day 0 · WhatsApp + email

    Blueprint link and PDF. "Your first milestone is in week 5. Here's what it is."

  2. Day 1 · WhatsApp

    A 10-minute tutorial for project #1 in their Blueprint.

  3. Day 3 · Email

    A case study of someone with the same starting role and domain.

  4. Day 5 · WhatsApp

    A mini readiness challenge ("Raise your score by 10 points") or a live webinar invite for their track.

  5. Day 7 · WhatsApp

    The counsellor introduces themselves by name, with 3 slot links.

  6. Day 10 · both

    Their start-by month: "To switch by your 6-month goal, this is the month to begin." It points to the counsellor to confirm a cohort.

  7. Day 14 onward · email

    Fortnightly: new tagged content from Learn.

Triggers that jump the queue: a fees question in chat, two visits to Your start plan, or a return visit after 7+ days. Each of these alerts the counsellor right away. Stop rules: they book, enrol, or opt out. Caps: at most 1 WhatsApp a day and 3 a week. WhatsApp templates must be approved by Meta ahead of time. The nurture messages are "marketing" category and need opt-in.

AI cost per learner

The budget below is in tokens, the units AI models are billed by. One token is roughly ¾ of an English word. "In" is what we send the model: instructions, the learner's answers, our data. "Out" is what it writes back. The last column turns those tokens into rupees.

How the rupee figures were worked out: Anthropic's list prices per million tokens are Haiku 4.5 at $1 in and $5 out, and Sonnet 5 at $2 in and $10 out. Cached input is billed at about a tenth of the normal input price. I've assumed $1 = ₹88. The ranges allow for the model's extra reasoning tokens and for cache misses. For 1,000 verified leads, AI cost is roughly ₹8,000–12,000.

Not included: WhatsApp charges and OTP/SMS fees, which your BSP bills per message. Across the nurture journey these will probably cost more than the AI does, so get your BSP's rate card when you budget.

Cost controls: nothing is generated before OTP verification. Prompt caching covers the system prompt and knowledge base. Persona-level caching means the roles and skills for a common combination (e.g. "QA · 4–6 yrs · BFSI · switch") are computed once and personalised on top. The profile is locked and the chat is metered.

Honesty guardrail: the model returns IDs for tracks, projects, testimonials, and salary rows, never raw figures. A validator rejects any output that contains a number or a company name not present in the supplied data, then retries once and falls back to a template.

Before build starts

Decisions I need from you

  1. Track list and curriculum-to-skill map. The coverage score, the core of the pitch, can't be built without it.
  2. Salary data source. Learnbay placement data by role, experience, and city, plus one named market report. Both are shown with a date on the page.
  3. Testimonial library. Consented stories tagged by starting role, domain, and outcome. We need at least 3 per major persona.
  4. Stack. Which CRM, WhatsApp BSP, and email tool are live today?
  5. Consent wording. Legal needs to approve the single-button consent at the unlock step against DPDP requirements.
  6. Correction policy. My recommendation is no self-serve regeneration, with every correction routed through a counsellor. Confirm, or allow one regeneration within 24 hours.
